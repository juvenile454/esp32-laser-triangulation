Line laser · requires the transistor driver

Without the transistor driver the laser sits permanently on the 5 V rail and these buttons have no effect. The viewer switches the laser off after the maximum on-time (– s), when this page stops polling for – s, and when it stops. Keep the manual switch within reach.


The focus value is relative: with the same scene and lighting, higher usually means sharper. Fix the field of view and working height first, then focus. Test colours one at a time and switch the LED off before laser work.
